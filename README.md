Description

**Never Wonder How Long Your Flight Will Take Again!**

**FlyTravelTimes** displays a real-time progress bar showing exactly how much time remains on your flight path. No more alt-tabbing or guessing - just a clean countdown timer that appears automatically when you fly.

**🌍 NOW IN 6 LANGUAGES!** Automatic detection - speaks your language!

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Automatic Flight Detection
- Detects when you start a flight path
- Calculates duration based on your destination
- No configuration needed - works right out of the box!

### 📊 Beautiful Progress Bar
- Real-time countdown timer (MM:SS format)
- Visual progress bar that fills as you fly
- Smooth animated spark effect
- Shows percentage completion
- Clean, unobtrusive design

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
Speaks YOUR language automatically!
- 🇬🇧 **English**

- 🇪🇸 **Spanish** (Español)
- 🇷🇺 **Russian** (Русский)
- 🇬🇷 **Greek** (Ελληνικά)

All messages, commands, and UI text are automatically displayed in your WoW client language!

## 💬 Commands

Simple slash commands (shown in your language):

```
/ftt                - Show help menu
/ftt toggle        - Enable/disable the addon
/ftt estimate      - Toggle flight time chat messages
/ftt test          - Test the timer bar (2-minute demo)
/ftt reset         - Reset bar position to center
```

---

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Install the addon** - Extract to your AddOns folder
2. **Take a flight** - That's it! The timer appears automatically
3. **Move the bar** - Left-click and drag to reposition
4. **Customize** - Use `/ftt` commands to adjust settings
5. **Enjoy in your language** - All text automatically translated!

## 📝 FAQ

**Q: What languages are supported?**
A: English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, and Greek! Your WoW client language is detected automatically.

**Q: Does it work on Classic and Retail?**
A: Yes! All versions from Classic Vanilla through Midnight (12.0).

**Q: Will it slow down my game?**
A: No. The addon is extremely lightweight with minimal performance impact. Multi-language support adds less than 10KB!

**Q: Can I move the timer bar?**
A: Absolutely! Just click and drag it anywhere. Your position is saved.

**Q: Does it support both factions?**
A: Yes! Horde and Alliance are both fully supported.

**Q: What if the time estimate is wrong?**
A: Flight times are based on the RXP database and are generally very accurate. Minor variations can occur due to server latency.

**Q: Does it work with Midnight (12.0)?**
A: Yes! The addon is fully updated with Midnight flight path data.

**Q: How do I change the language?**
A: You don't! It automatically matches your WoW client language. No configuration needed!
